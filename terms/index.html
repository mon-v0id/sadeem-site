Real conditions depend on local weather, haze, terrain, buildings, your eyesight, your equipment and many other things the app cannot know.

  • Forecasts come from Apple Weather and may be wrong.
  • Light-pollution values are estimated, not measured at your site.
  • A target shown as visible may not be visible to you, and the reverse.
  • Event times and dates are calculated and may differ slightly from official sources.

Do not rely on Sadeem for navigation, safety-critical, scientific or professional decisions.

4. Your safety

Never look at the Sun with your eyes, binoculars, a telescope or a camera without a proper certified solar filter, including during a partial or annular eclipse. Looking at the Sun can cause permanent blindness. Nothing in Sadeem tells you it is safe to look at the Sun.

Observing usually happens at night and outdoors. You are responsible for your own safety: tell someone where you are going, respect private property and local laws, take care near roads, water and uneven ground, dress for the weather, and do not use the app while driving.
