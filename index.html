Questions

What is the Stargazing Score?

A number from 0 to 100 that measures how dark and clear your sky is, hour by hour. It counts the darkness of your site, twilight and the Moon most, and cloud, rain and haze limit it. Places and nights are scored on one scale, so you can compare them.

Does Sadeem work in a city?

Yes. Sadeem estimates the light pollution where you are and only recommends what you can realistically see, such as the Moon, planets and bright stars and clusters. It also tells you how much more a darker site would show.

Do I need a telescope?

No. Sadeem plans for your eyes first, and also for binoculars and telescopes.

Does Sadeem collect my data?

No. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. Your journal stays on your device and in your own iCloud if you turn sync on.

Which languages does Sadeem support?

English, Arabic and Spanish, including a full right-to-left Arabic interface.
